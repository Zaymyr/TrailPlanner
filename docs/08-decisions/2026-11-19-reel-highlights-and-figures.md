# Reel Figures And Highlights Mode

## Status

Accepted (2026-11-19).

## Context

Two needs met in the admin reel generator. First, the reel showed two different totals for the same race. The badge "N KM" rounded `races.distance_km`, the live counter "KM x / N" rounded the GPX length, and the story, the aid cards and the AI inputs mixed both sources with their own rounding (integer in one place, one decimal in another). Nothing reconciled the race record with the GPX, so a 66.63 km race measured 64.9 km on its GPX could read "67 KM" on the badge and "/ 65" on the counter.

Second, a full-course flyover of an ultra lasts up to 2-3 minutes; a short "temps forts" reel (30-45 s) suits Instagram better.

## Decision

1. **The race record is the announced truth.** `races.distance_km` and `races.elevation_gain_m` drive the badge, the intro, the counter total and the race figures sent to the script and caption writers.
2. **The GPX is rescaled to it.** Every GPX kilometre shown (counter, legs, caption ranges, profile ticks, aid cards, script `deKm`/`aKm`, stations flown over) is multiplied by race km ÷ GPX km, and every GPX D+ by race D+ ÷ GPX D+. A race figure is trusted within ±15 % of the GPX length (±35 % for D+, which depends far more on smoothing); otherwise, or when it is missing, the GPX figure is used everywhere (badge included) and the editor logs a warning. Grades and D− stay as measured on the GPX. Organizer station kilometres are in the announced scale and are converted once to place stations on the GPX.
3. **One rounding policy**, in `apps/web/app/admin/video-generator/race-figures.ts`: total distance in whole kilometres from 10 km (one decimal below); positions and partial lengths with one decimal (",0" dropped in text, kept on the live counter); every D+ computed from the GPX to the nearest 10 m; the announced race D+ as announced (it is the truth, not a measurement, so it is not re-rounded).

4. **Highlights mode behind `storyMode`.** `VideoCompositionSettings.storyMode` is `"full"` (default: the existing flyover, unchanged) or `"highlights"`. Highlights mode keeps 3 to 5 deterministic moments (`highlights.ts`), dwells on each and jumps between them in about 1.2 s, aims at 30-45 s and is capped at 50 s; the script route sizes words for that length and keeps the no-invented-numbers rule.
5. **Narrations keyed by (race_id, mode).** Each mode has its own beat ids (highlights use `hl-…`, intro and outro included), so a race gets one saved narration per mode: migration `20261119120000_add_video_narrations_mode.sql` moves `video_narrations` from a `race_id` key to `(race_id, mode)` with `mode in ('full','highlights')`, existing rows become `full`, the full clips keep `<race_id>.json` and highlights clips use `<race_id>.highlights.json`. The narration routes take `?mode=` (`full` when absent).

6. **Subtitles (phase 3).** The burned-in subtitles are groups of 2 to 5 words (`subtitle-groups.ts`, both story modes) instead of 60-character lines, cut only at natural boundaries: punctuation, a pause over 250 ms between words when start times exist, otherwise between phrases (before a preposition or conjunction). A group never ends on an article, contraction, preposition, determiner, conjunction or clitic (`FRENCH_FUNCTION_WORDS`, `'` and `’`) nor on a bare numeral, never crosses a sentence end, is never a singleton (unless the sentence is one word; a trailing singleton merges into the previous group, up to 6 words), and keeps number + unit ("5 520 m D+", "13 %", "18h12") and consecutive capitalised names ("La Comelle", "Hivernale des Templiers") together. Instagram safe zone: a fixed-width box (x 6 % to 85 %, text centred, one line preferred, two at most, 4.2 cqmin = 45 px at 1080) centred at 74 % of the frame height (64 % at first, which read as mid-screen on a phone), so its bottom is at most about 1,489 px of 1920 (assert: 1,500); the Mapbox attribution is not moved. **Optional start times:** ElevenLabs `character_start_times_seconds` are read with the same length check and spoken-to-displayed mapping as the end times and stored as `characterStartMs`, optional in the saved clip and in the render snapshot, so old narrations and snapshots parse and render (pause detection then falls back to punctuation, timing to the end times). The sober karaoke word highlight was not built.

7. **Hook (phase 2).** The first 2.4 s of a reel stop the scroll instead of showing a title card over a static overview. Setting `hookIntro` (null = on for highlights, off for full; the full mode keeps its intro exactly unless it is switched on). From frame 0 the camera already flies the hardest climb (else the first moment) at 58° from the vertical, 3 to 8 km away, orbiting 14° while moving forward, then glides into the chase camera of the first moment through the existing smoothed camera track. The overlay shows one headline figure on one line (at least 14 cqmin; it shrinks rather than wraps) and, from 0.5 to 0.9 s, the short race name, inside the Instagram safe zone; the first moment's caption follows at 2.4 s. Headline figure rule (`pickShockFigure`): 100 km or more → the distance (an ultra length is the most striking fact: « 185 km » for L'Ultra des Druides); otherwise a D+ of at least 30 m/km → the D+ (a clearly hilly course: « 2 276 m D+ » for Astragale Trail, 34 m/km); otherwise the distance. It replaces the suggested « D+ ≥ 3,000 m or ≥ 35 m/km » rule, which put 5,520 m D+ before 185 km for a 30 m/km ultra and 67 km before 2,276 m D+ on a 34 m/km course. The short name is `race_events.short_name` (new nullable column, migration `20261119150000_add_race_events_short_name.sql`, 1-40 characters once trimmed, no « … »; edited by admins in the catalog event dialog) or derived from the event name without year, marks, edition marker or sponsor; never truncated. The hook sentence keeps the `hl-intro` id; the prompt now asks for a question or a strong statement of 12 words at most without unprovided figures.

8. **Light phone overlay (phase 4).** Setting `compactOverlay` (null = on for highlights, off for full). The top third keeps only the current kilometre, the profile and the leg card: no title band (title, subtitle, km / D+ pill), no profile ticks, aid bubbles reduced to the station name with its figures and cutoff moved to the leg card, and the band moved down to 12 cqmin (below Instagram's top bar). In both layouts every readable text is at least 3 cqmin = 32.4 px at 1080 wide (`OVERLAY_FONT_CQMIN`, tested); only the Mapbox attribution stays smaller.

9. **Camera and labels (phase 5).** Setting `tiltedCamera` (null = on for highlights, off for full). The chase camera tilts to a Mapbox pitch of 60° (55-65°, was about 36°) behind the runner, bearing from the leg direction with a 12° side offset, terrain clearance kept (it steps back before it rises). `camera-framing.ts` guarantees by a pinhole projection that the runner and the next 2 km stay in a safe rectangle of the frame (below the leg card at 56 cqmin, above the bottom 20 %, 6 % side margins), by pulling back, turning or moving the raw pose before the smoothing; tilted mode only, so the full flyover without the setting is exactly the previous camera. No framing is promised during the 1.2 s jump between two moments. Map labels (both modes, `label-layout.ts`): a loop (under 1,500 m) shows one « Départ / Arrivée » label with the start name; labels are hidden when cut by the frame edge or when they overlap a more important one (shown aid bubble, then start/finish, then others), computed from `map.project` and cached card sizes. The dedicated brand-style environment variable was dropped: the existing brand style setting is enough.

10. **Single text content (phase 7).** In highlights mode one Claude call (`POST /api/admin/video-generator/content`, same model, refusal fallback and max_tokens as the script route, shared prompt blocks in `lib/video-script-prompt.ts`) replaces the script call and the caption call. Output validated by Zod: `{ hook, script_segments: [{ highlight_id, text }], cta, caption, hashtags, cover_text }`. Server-side limits: hook ≤ 12 words, cover text ≤ 6 words, 3 to 5 hashtags once normalised (Instagram accepts 5 per post since late 2025; the first version asked for 8 to 15), caption ≤ 2,200 characters, one segment per beat id sent within its word budget (unknown ids dropped), `hl-intro` starting with the hook. **Anti-invention:** the prompt receives only facts and forbids any figure not provided, and `findUnprovidedNumbers` (pure, tested, French number formats) checks every text field against the numbers of the input; a limit or number failure triggers one low-effort revision, then the remaining problems are returned as warnings shown in the editor, never silently fixed. **Storage:** the JSON is stored with the video: `video_narrations.content` (reloaded with the race and mode) and a snapshot in `video_render_jobs.content` at render creation (migration `20261119160000_add_reel_content.sql`, two additive nullable columns, RLS and grants unchanged). **Publications:** a draft made from a job with content is pre-filled with its caption, hashtags and cover text (all editable) and copies the full JSON into `social_posts.content`; without content the plain template stays. `cover_url` stays unset (Instagram uses the first frame), the cover text is only stored. The full mode keeps its two calls (script, then caption) and has no content. **Caption voice (2026-11-20):** the caption is written to be read under the reel, not reread from the voice-over: same lively tone as the video (tutoiement, trail vocabulary), short lines, a hook line, a question for comments and the call to action (plan de course in the app, lien en bio), with 2 to 8 emojis (`countEmojis`, joined sequences count once). `sanitizeReelContent` flags a flat caption (fewer than 2 emojis), an emoji burst (more than 8) and any run of 7 words shared with the hook or a narration segment other than `hl-outro` (`findCopiedPhrase`, accents and punctuation ignored); each triggers the revision, then a warning.

11. **Audio and end screen (phase 6b).** The soundtrack is mastered by a pure JS module (`audio-master.ts`): BS.1770-4 integrated loudness and 4x true peak, one linear gain to -14 LUFS with a true peak of at most -1 dBTP (no limiter; when the ceiling wins, the report says the mix stays quieter). It runs the same in the browser export and the Cloud Run render because no step depends on Web Audio automation or ffmpeg. An optional music bed (`musicTrack`: none, auto from the race id, or a manifest id; manifest in `public/reel-music/`, shipped empty, royalty-free licence required) is looped or trimmed, set 8 LU under the voice (voice -16 LUFS, music -24 LUFS), ducked 10 dB under the voice with a 150 ms attack and 400 ms release, faded 0.5 s in and 1.5 s out. The end screen lasts `max(2.5 s, gap + CTA voice + 2 s hold)`; in highlights it shows « Ton plan pour cette course est prêt » / « Dans l’appli Pace Yourself » / « Lien en bio » inside the Instagram safe zone, and the full mode keeps its card. Tests assert the voice never ends within 0.5 s of the video end.

12. **One-click generation.** « Générer le reel » replaces the nine manual clicks by one for the common case. (a) **Pipeline:** text (`/content`), voice (`/voice`), narration save, then the background render, in that order, on the same functions as the individual buttons (extracted as `requestScript`, `applyHighlightsContent`, `requestVoiceClips`, `putNarration` and `launchBackgroundRender`), with the data of each step handed to the next explicitly rather than read back from React state. A failing step stops the chain and « Reprendre » resumes from it; the run can be cancelled until the render starts; the text step's warnings are shown and never block. (b) **Highlights is the UI default**, set in `INITIAL_EDITOR_SETTINGS` only: the Zod default of `storyMode` stays `full`, so old render snapshots and saved settings without `storyMode` parse as before (decision 4 is unchanged). (c) **Automatic draft:** `video_render_jobs.auto_draft` (set by the render-jobs POST `autoDraft`, only with `content`) makes the render completion route create the job's `social_posts` draft, never scheduled or published, owned by the job's creator, with the service role the callback already uses, so no auth, RLS or grant change. It is idempotent and non-fatal: an existing automatic draft is returned first, a partial unique index on `social_posts (video_job_id) where auto_generated` backs it up (this is why the migration also adds `social_posts.auto_generated`, which also lets Publications show a precise « Généré automatiquement » badge instead of inferring it), and a failure is logged without payload and leaves the job `done`. Human control stays at publication: the admin reviews the draft in Publications and publishes or schedules it. Alternatives rejected: creating the draft from the browser when it sees the job done (needs the tab open, defeats the background render); inferring the badge from `content` plus the job flag (also matches a manual draft of the same render).
13. **Pool de voix.** The reel's voice was remembered per browser only, so every reel sounded the same. (a) **Shared pool:** the admin keeps 4 or 5 ElevenLabs voices in `video_voices` (additive, service-role-only table like `video_render_jobs`: RLS on, no policy, client privileges revoked; unique ElevenLabs id, name 1-40, `active`), managed from the generator's voice settings through admin routes gated by `authorizeVideoAdmin` and validated with Zod. (b) **Random pick:** by default (« Au hasard parmi N voix actives ») a new reel, whether from the button or the one-click pipeline, draws one active voice with the pure `pickPoolVoice`, leaving out the voice of the race's previous saved narration when at least two are active so consecutive reels vary; re-generating the voice of an existing narration keeps its saved voice unless the admin clicks « Changer de voix »; a specific pool voice or the free id (« Autre ID ») bypass the draw. (c) **Saved with the narration:** `voiceId` and `voiceName` go into the existing `video_narrations.voice` jsonb (no schema change) and are handed from the pipeline's voice step to its save step through the context, never read back from React state. (d) **Empty pool:** falls back to the free id, then `ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID`, then the premade default, so nothing changes until a voice is added. (e) **Import:** a one-time button copies the named voices of the browser library into the pool. Alternatives rejected: an env var list of voices (needs a deploy to change); drawing the voice on the server in the voice route (the editor must know and save the voice, and the voice route stays a plain text-to-speech call); a per-race fixed voice (reels of one race would never vary).

15. **Automatisation (phase 9).** A calendar of reels is planned from the catalog and, behind two switches off by default, generated without the browser. (a) **Plan:** the pure `planReels` (`lib/reel-planner.ts`) keeps live solo formats (no `relais|relay|duo|team`) with a GPX, at least one aid station, 30 km or more and a future date; 30-60 km get a full reel, longer ones highlights. Per edition the formats publish shortest to longest, the longest at event start − 7 days at the latest and each shorter one at least 7 days before its longer sibling's actual day; at most 3 reels per Monday-Sunday week and 1 per day, assigned latest-possible, editions in event order, longest first. A format with no valid day from today + 3 days (generation lead) is skipped « Trop tard — à faire à la main », never compressed. (b) **Duplicate guard:** a race with a non-failed render or post created from its date − 180 days is never processed automatically, checked when planning and again before the text and before the render; `unique (race_id, edition_key)` forbids a second item. (c) **Server generation:** the content, script, caption and voice route cores move to `lib/reel-writer.ts` and `lib/reel-voice.ts` (routes unchanged in behaviour) and the race read to `lib/video-generator-race.ts`; `reel-composition.ts` rebuilds figures, beats, writer requests and the render snapshot with the editor's pure modules, so the render page replays a server snapshot exactly like a browser one (round-trip test). Full reels get a content JSON assembled from their script and caption so the automatic draft works for both modes. (d) **Executor:** pg_cron calls `/api/cron/reel-automation` hourly; a tick recomputes the plan, follows renders, and runs one idempotent step of one due item under a lease, with 3 attempts. (e) **Switches:** « Génération automatique » creates drafts only (human review stays); « Publication automatique » additionally schedules each draft at its planned slot at 18:00 Paris, which lifts the human-validation guardrail of decision 12 by an explicit, confirmed admin choice; dry-run mode still applies. Alternatives rejected: driving a headless editor to build the snapshot (heavy, and the render page already proves the pure path); compressing late editions into fewer days (the user asked for skips); a Vercel cron (Hobby plan, daily only).

16. **Carrousels (2026-11-20).** Three weekly Instagram carousel series built from the catalog: « Le plan chiffré » (Tuesday), « Le brief » (Thursday), « La semaine qui vient » (Sunday), each behind its own switch next to the reel switches, which also drive their generation and publication. (a) **Real numbers only:** the three objectives are the public course page's reference levels (5:00 / 6:30 / 8:00 min/km flat through `estimateCourseTime`, rounded to whole hours from 5 h), carbs per hour the public carb calculator with tolerance 75 / 50 / 25 per level (validated by the maintainer), water and sodium the onboarding estimate (pace-independent, so shown once under the table), section grams the middle objective's rate × estimated section duration, allocated to add up exactly; the sentence under the table only states what the figures show; a race without distance, D+ or a computable time is skipped, never filled in. (b) **Never a fake profile:** the band shows the real GPX profile, else a flat path with the aid stations at their kilometre and decor mountains whose steepness follows D+/km, else kilometre stones; the sky follows the race clock. (c) **Livret signal:** a paid edition entitlement (source Stripe or bank transfer, never an admin or complimentary grant) with a RaceBook tier (Essentiel, Complet, Signature) and the format's RaceBook live and visible. (d) **Rendering:** a new service-role table `carousel_render_jobs` rather than a kind on `video_render_jobs` (whose rows count as reels and need a race and an MP4); the existing Cloud Run job opens `/render/carousel/<id>` with a carousel-scoped token, captures one strip with `html-to-image` and uploads JPEG slides. (e) **Publishing:** `social_posts.media_type` (`reel` default) and the carousel flow inside the same publisher state machine. (f) **Planning:** at most one post a day across reels and carousels: reels avoid the weekdays of the enabled series; a carousel slot whose day is taken, without a candidate or too close is skipped with its reason; one plan and one brief per edition, never after a post of the same series. Alternatives rejected: rendering slides with an SVG-to-JPEG library on Vercel (fonts and layout would diverge from the validated HTML maquettes); a separate render worker (the existing job already gives headless Chrome and the bucket); compressing missed weeks (a missed week is simply skipped). Details: [Automatic Instagram Carousels](../05-integrations/instagram-carousels.md).

17. **Reels as Instagram shows them (2026-11-20).** After the first live publications: (a) the feed shows a reel cropped to 4:5 (y 285 to 1635 of 1920) without any interface, so the subtitle box is now centred (x 13 % to 87 %, it leaned left) and lower (centre 77.5 %, still above the Reels caption at y 1570), and the light profile band starts at 28 cqmin, below the crop (it was cut off at 12 cqmin); the route framing keeps the band out (capped at 40 % of the height). (b) The hook names the format (« Marathon de l'Orchis ») in large type and the event under it; a generic format name keeps the event alone. (c) Map detail: exports lay the frame out at the video size ÷ 2 at density 2 instead of the preview size at 2.67, so Mapbox loads the zoom level made for 1080p (paths, contour lines, streams; checked side by side on the same free camera, which frames identically), the fixed pixel sizes are scaled to keep their look, and the 1080p bitrate is 16 Mb/s. Alternatives rejected: a raster tile-size override (the Pace Yourself style imports its basemap, there is no raster source to tune); supersampling at 4K (four times the GPU work for less gain than the right tile level).

18. **One click in both modes (2026-11-20).** « Générer le reel » was highlights only, while the reel rules make every format up to 60 km a full-course reel. The admin now picks the mode above the button (the editor story mode, advised one marked ★); a full reel chains `/script`, `/caption`, the voice, the save and the render, its stored text assembled by `assembleFullReelContent` like the automatic ones, so its Instagram draft is pre-filled too.

## Consequences

- Badge, counter, voice, captions, aid cards and AI inputs show the same numbers; the counter ends exactly on the badge total.
- Internal geometry (camera, story cuts, timeline, race clock) stays in GPX kilometres; only display and AI inputs convert. Code that formats a kilometre or a D+ locally reintroduces the bug.
- Aid stations move slightly along the GPX when the race record and the GPX disagree (by the scale factor), so a station sits where its organizer kilometre says relative to the announced total.
- A wrong GPX (or a typo in the race record) beyond the tolerance is not hidden: the GPX wins and the console says why; fixing the data is a catalog task.
- The full mode keeps its timeline and camera behaviour; highlights mode adds its own branch (fixed transits, tighter smoothing, local-speed camera distance) rather than retuning the shared one.
- Highlight ids derive from GPX kilometres (like leg ids), so a GPX replacement re-keys their texts, as it already does for full legs.
- Switching to a mode for the first time restores that mode's saved composition settings too (they decide its beat ids).
- Subtitle timing needs no migration: clips saved before keep working with end times only; regenerating the voice adds start times.
- A hooked reel spends no time on the run-up to its first moment and its first moment absorbs the end of the hook sentence: the fixture reels last 30 s and 39 s (41 s without the hook). Render snapshots saved before the hook lack `hookIntro`, so a highlights snapshot replayed afterwards opens on the hook.
- Highlights texts come from one call, so the caption and the script cannot disagree on a fact; the cost is one larger structured output and a revision that can fail (the draft and its warnings are kept). Content is null for full reels and for rows saved before phase 7, and every consumer works without it.
- `race_events.short_name` is read only by admin service-role routes; RLS, grants and public reads are unchanged.
- The full layout gets larger small texts (ticks, chips, bubbles, place labels) but keeps its elements; the light layout is the highlights default.
- The previous web version upserts on `race_id` alone: between the migration and the web deploy of the release, its saves fail (reads keep working).
- Without a manifest track the reel has no music but is still loudness-mastered, so its sound level changes compared with phase 7 exports (normalised to -14 LUFS).
- Opening the generator on Temps forts changes what an admin sees first, not what is stored: a race with a saved « Parcours complet » narration still loads it when that mode is chosen.
- A render launched by one click costs a GPU render without a human review of the text first; the warnings and the Publications review are the guard, and the draft is never published automatically.
- The render time shown (≈ 10 min) is a fixed estimate, not a measurement.
- A deactivated or deleted pool voice stays in the saved narrations that used it (the voice id and name are copied there), so older reels are unaffected; the table check accepts ids of 8-64 characters while the voice route accepts 10-40, narrower than real ElevenLabs ids (20).
- Automatic reels use the default composition (forced to 1080p, since the reel goes to Instagram as is) and voice settings, never an admin's browser preferences; races whose GPX has no elevation stay manual. With « Publication automatique » on, a reel can reach Instagram without anyone reading it: the warning in Publications and the dry-run mode are the remaining guards.
- Carousels take three weekdays from the reels when their series are on; the reel caps (3 a week) then fit in the four remaining days. The plan chiffré g/h depend on a tolerance mapping per runner level that is a calibration choice, not a published rule.
- The outro is about 2 s longer than before (voice plus a 2 s hold); the 50 s cap on highlights is unchanged and the flyover absorbs it when the total is clamped.
